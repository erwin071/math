## AMERICAN GOVERNMENT 美國政府

### A: Principles of American Government 美國政府基本原則

**1. What is the form of government of the United States?**
**美國的政府形式是什麼？**
- Republic 共和國
- Constitution-based federal republic 以憲法為基礎的聯邦共和國
- Representative democracy 代議民主制

**2. What is the supreme law of the land? ★**
**國家的最高法律是什麼？**
- (U.S.) Constitution （美國）憲法

**3. Name one thing the U.S. Constitution does.**
**列出一件美國憲法做的事。**
- Forms the government 建立政府
- Defines powers of government 界定政府的權力
- Defines the parts of government 界定政府的組成部分
- Protects the rights of the people 保障人民的權利

**4. The U.S. Constitution starts with the words "We the People." What does "We the People" mean?**
**美國憲法以「We the People」開頭，「We the People」是什麼意思？**
- Self-government 自治
- Popular sovereignty 人民主權
- Consent of the governed 被治者的同意
- People should govern themselves 人民應該自己治理自己
- (Example of) social contract 社會契約（的例子）

**5. How are changes made to the U.S. Constitution?**
**美國憲法如何修改？**
- Amendments 修正案
- The amendment process 修憲程序

**6. What does the Bill of Rights protect?**
**《權利法案》保護什麼？**
- (The basic) rights of Americans 美國人的（基本）權利
- (The basic) rights of people living in the United States 居住在美國人民的（基本）權利

**7. How many amendments does the U.S. Constitution have? ★**
**美國憲法有多少條修正案？**
- Twenty-seven (27) 二十七條（27）

**8. Why is the Declaration of Independence important?**
**為什麼《獨立宣言》很重要？**
- It says America is free from British control. 它宣告美國脫離英國統治
- It says all people are created equal. 它宣示人人生而平等
- It identifies inherent rights. 它確立了天賦人權
- It identifies individual freedoms. 它確立了個人自由

**9. What founding document said the American colonies were free from Britain?**
**哪份建國文獻宣告美洲殖民地脫離英國而獨立？**
- Declaration of Independence 《獨立宣言》

**10. Name two important ideas from the Declaration of Independence and the U.S. Constitution.**
**說出《獨立宣言》和美國憲法中的兩項重要思想。**
- Equality 平等
- Liberty 自由
- Social contract 社會契約
- Natural rights 自然權利
- Limited government 有限政府
- Self-government 自治

**11. The words "Life, Liberty, and the pursuit of Happiness" are in what founding document?**
**「生命、自由和追求幸福」出現在哪份建國文獻中？**
- Declaration of Independence 《獨立宣言》

**12. What is the economic system of the United States? ★**
**美國的經濟制度是什麼？**
- Capitalism 資本主義
- Free market economy 自由市場經濟

**13. What is the rule of law?**
**什麼是法治？**
- Everyone must follow the law. 人人都必須遵守法律
- Leaders must obey the law. 領導者必須服從法律
- Government must obey the law. 政府必須服從法律
- No one is above the law. 沒有人能凌駕於法律之上

**14. Many documents influenced the U.S. Constitution. Name one.**
**許多文獻影響了美國憲法，舉出一個。**
- Declaration of Independence 《獨立宣言》
- Articles of Confederation 《邦聯條例》
- Federalist Papers 《聯邦黨人文集》
- Anti-Federalist Papers 《反聯邦黨人文集》
- Virginia Declaration of Rights 維吉尼亞權利宣言
- Fundamental Orders of Connecticut 康乃狄克基本法令
- Mayflower Compact 《五月花號公約》
- Iroquois Great Law of Peace 易洛魁和平大法

**15. There are three branches of government. Why?**
**政府有三個分支，為什麼？**
- So one part does not become too powerful 以免某一部分權力過大
- Checks and balances 制衡
- Separation of powers 權力分立

### B: System of Government 政府體制

**16. Name the three branches of government.**
**說出政府的三個分支。**
- Legislative, executive, and judicial 立法、行政、司法
- Congress, president, and the courts 國會、總統、法院

**17. The President of the United States is in charge of which branch of government?**
**美國總統負責哪個政府分支？**

- Legislative branch 立法部門

### C: Rights and Responsibilities 權利與責任

**63. There are four amendments to the U.S. Constitution about who can vote. Describe one of them.**
**美國憲法有四條關於投票權的修正案，描述其中一條。**
- Citizens eighteen (18) and older (can vote). 年滿 18 歲的公民可以投票
- You don't have to pay (a poll tax) to vote. 投票不必繳納人頭稅
- Any citizen can vote. (Women and men can vote.) 任何公民（男女）都可以投票
- A male citizen of any race (can vote). 任何種族的男性公民可以投票

**64. Who can vote in federal elections, run for federal office, and serve on a jury in the United States?**
**在美國，誰可以參加聯邦選舉投票、競選聯邦公職並擔任陪審員？**
- Citizens 公民
- Citizens of the United States 美國公民
- U.S. citizens 美國公民

**65. What are three rights of everyone living in the United States?**
**住在美國的每個人擁有三項什麼權利？**
- Freedom of expression 表達自由
- Freedom of speech 言論自由
- Freedom of assembly 集會自由
- Freedom to petition the government 向政府請願的自由
- Freedom of religion 宗教自由
- The right to bear arms 持有武器的權利

**66. What do we show loyalty to when we say the Pledge of Allegiance? ★**
**宣誓效忠誓詞時，我們向什麼表達忠誠？**
- The United States 美國
- The flag 美國國旗

**67. Name two promises that new citizens make in the Oath of Allegiance.**
**說出新公民在效忠宣誓中做出的兩項承諾。**
- Give up loyalty to other countries 放棄對其他國家的忠誠
- Defend the (U.S.) Constitution 捍衛（美國）憲法
- Obey the laws of the United States 遵守美國法律
- Serve in the military (if needed) 需要時從軍服役
- Serve (help, do important work for) the nation (if needed) 需要時為國家服務
- Be loyal to the United States 忠於美國

**68. How can people become United States citizens?**
**人們如何成為美國公民？**
- Be born in the United States, under the conditions set by the 14th Amendment 依據第 14 修正案規定的條件在美國出生
- Naturalize 歸化入籍
- Derive citizenship (under conditions set by Congress) 依國會規定取得衍生公民身分

**69. What are two examples of civic participation in the United States?**
**舉出兩個美國公民參與的例子。**
- Vote 投票
- Run for office 競選公職
- Join a political party 加入政黨
- Help with a campaign 協助競選活動
- Join a civic group 加入公民團體
- Join a community group 加入社區團體
- Give an elected official your opinion (on an issue) 就公共議題向民選官員表達意見
- Contact elected officials 聯繫民選官員
- Support or oppose an issue or policy 支持或反對某項議題或政策
- Write to a newspaper 寫信給報社

**70. What is one way Americans can serve their country?**
**舉出一種美國人服務國家（為國效力）的方式。**
- Vote 投票
- Pay taxes 納稅
- Obey the law 遵守法律
- Serve in the military 從軍服役
- Run for office 競選公職
- Work for local, state, or federal government 為地方、州或聯邦政府工作

**71. Why is it important to pay federal taxes?**
**為什麼繳納聯邦稅很重要？**
- Required by law 法律規定
- All people pay to fund the federal government 人人都納稅以支應聯邦政府運作
- Required by the (U.S.) Constitution (16th Amendment) 憲法（第 16 修正案）規定
- Civic duty 公民義務

**72. It is important for all men age 18 through 25 to register for the Selective Service. Name one reason why.**
**為什麼 18 至 25 歲的所有男性都應該登記兵役徵召？**
- Required by law 法律規定
- Civic duty 公民義務
- Makes the draft fair, if needed 需要時使徵兵更公平
