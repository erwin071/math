Grade 4 Math Practice Worksheet
(Mid-Level · ICPS Preparation)
================================================

PART 1: Whole Number Operations & Number Sense
------------------------------------------------

1. Calculate:  456 × 7 − 389 = ?

2. Find the pattern and fill in the blank:
   3, 7, 15, 31, ___, 127

3. How many integers from 1 to 50 are divisible by BOTH 3 and 4?


PART 2: Factors & Multiples
------------------------------------------------

4. List ALL the factors of 36.
   How many factors does 36 have in total?

5. Marcus has some candies. When he tries to share them equally among
   4 friends, 6 friends, or 9 friends, there are no leftovers.
   What is the LEAST number of candies he could have?


PART 3: Fractions & Decimals
------------------------------------------------

6. Calculate:  3/4 + 5/6 = ?
   (Write your answer as a simplified fraction.)

7. A rope is 2.4 meters long. After cutting off 3/8 of it,
   how many meters are left?


PART 4: Geometry & Area
------------------------------------------------

8. A rectangle is 12 cm long and 8 cm wide.
   A circle with radius 3 cm is cut out from the rectangle.
   What is the remaining area? (Use pi = 3.14)

9. A right triangle has one angle of 90 degrees and another angle of 35 degrees.
   What is the measure of the third angle?


PART 5: Logic & Word Problems
------------------------------------------------

10. There are red, yellow, and blue balls.
    - Red balls are 5 more than yellow balls.
    - Yellow balls are twice as many as blue balls.
    - The total number of all three colors is 45.
    How many blue balls are there?

11. Emma goes jogging every morning.
    On Day 1 she runs 200 meters.
    Each day she runs 50 meters more than the day before.
    a) How far does she run on Day 7?
    b) What is the total distance she runs over 7 days?


PART 6: Challenge (Optional)
------------------------------------------------

12. A two-digit number has the following properties:
    - The units digit is 3 more than the tens digit.
    - The product of the two digits equals twice the sum of the two digits.
    What is this two-digit number?


================================================
ANSWER KEY
================================================

1.  456 x 7 = 3,192
    3,192 - 389 = 2,803
    Answer: 2,803

2.  Pattern: each term = previous term x 2 + 1
    3 -> 7 -> 15 -> 31 -> 63 -> 127
    Answer: 63

3.  Must be divisible by LCM(3, 4) = 12
    Multiples of 12 from 1 to 50: 12, 24, 36, 48
    Answer: 4 numbers

4.  Factors of 36: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, 12, 18, 36
    Answer: 9 factors

5.  LCM(4, 6, 9):
    4 = 2^2,  6 = 2 x 3,  9 = 3^2
    LCM = 2^2 x 3^2 = 36
    Answer: 36 candies

6.  3/4 + 5/6
    Common denominator = 12
    = 9/12 + 10/12 = 19/12
    Answer: 19/12  (or 1 and 7/12)

7.  Cut off: 2.4 x (3/8) = 0.9 m
    Remaining: 2.4 - 0.9 = 1.5 m
    Answer: 1.5 meters

8.  Rectangle area: 12 x 8 = 96 cm^2
    Circle area: 3.14 x 3^2 = 3.14 x 9 = 28.26 cm^2
    Remaining: 96 - 28.26 = 67.74 cm^2
    Answer: 67.74 cm^2

9.  Sum of angles in a triangle = 180 degrees
    Third angle: 180 - 90 - 35 = 55 degrees
    Answer: 55 degrees

10. Let blue = b
    yellow = 2b
    red = 2b + 5
    Total: b + 2b + (2b + 5) = 5b + 5 = 45
    5b = 40
    b = 8
    Answer: 8 blue balls
    (Check: blue=8, yellow=16, red=21 -> 8+16+21 = 45 ✓)

11. a) Day 7: 200 + (7 - 1) x 50 = 200 + 300 = 500 meters
    b) Total (arithmetic series):
       Sum = (number of terms) / 2 x (first term + last term)
           = 7/2 x (200 + 500)
           = 3.5 x 700
           = 2,450 meters
    Answer: a) 500 meters    b) 2,450 meters total

12. Let tens digit = t,  units digit = t + 3
    Product of digits = t x (t + 3)
    Sum of digits = t + (t + 3) = 2t + 3
    Condition: t(t + 3) = 2 x (2t + 3)
    t^2 + 3t = 4t + 6
    t^2 - t - 6 = 0
    (t - 3)(t + 2) = 0
    t = 3  (t = -2 is rejected since digits must be positive)
    Units digit = 3 + 3 = 6
    Answer: 36
    (Check: product = 3 x 6 = 18;  sum = 3 + 6 = 9;  18 = 2 x 9 ✓)
